The Qwel canvas implementation uses its own cached Core Image pipeline and
editable layers. Its rounded-distance-field refraction, saturation and rim-light
approach is informed by the public LiquidGlass shader. No hierarchy capture,
continuous screenshot timer, shader noise or private UIKit APIs are included.
